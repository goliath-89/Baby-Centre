Recommendation: freemium with a subscription and a lifetime option. Logging stays free forever, which is what earns good reviews; Premium sells the things that save time and worry. Change any number below and the totals update.

Trade-offs

  • Subscription vs one-time. Subscriptions fund updates and sync costs but put some parents off; a lifetime option answers that. A single one-time price (about RM 29.90) would need about 500 buyers every year, with no renewals to build on.
  • Free tier size. Generous free logging brings reviews and word of mouth but lowers conversion. A hard paywall converts more per download (about 10% of trials) but drags ratings in this category.
  • Your time. Expect 3–6 hours a week: support emails, store reviews, phone OS updates each September, and small releases. That is the real cost.
  • Accounts and data. Selling the app means running your own backend: sign-in, sync, backups, account deletion inside the app (Apple requires it) and a privacy policy under the Personal Data Protection Act.
  • Health claims. Growth readings and the cry helper must stay "guidance, not a diagnosis". Medical claims invite store rejection and liability.
  • Malaysia only vs worldwide. 396,175 births a year is a small pool; English listings in Singapore, the Philippines, the UK and Australia multiply reach at local prices, while the KKM schedule stays a Malaysian advantage.

2. A new name and look

"Baby Centre" is generic and close to the large BabyCenter brand, so it would be hard to find and risky to trademark. The new name should be short, warm, easy to say in English and Malay, and free in the stores.

RecommendedBelai

Membelai: to cuddle, stroke and care for. Two syllables, local roots, sounds soft in any language. No baby app found with this name.

Tagline: "Calm care, together."

AlternativeTumbuh

"To grow." Strong for growth and milestones, but harder to say outside Malaysia and Indonesia.

Tagline: "Every feed, nap and milestone."

AvoidDodoi

The Malay lullaby word is lovely, but it is very close to "Doudou: Baby Tracker for Two", an existing app for the same job.

Before committing: search MyIPO and the stores, and check belai.app, belai.my and the social handles.

3. What earns five stars

Top-rated trackers win on the same few things: logging in one tap at night, nothing ever lost, sharing that just works, and a fair free tier. Reviews punish paywalls on basics, lost data and ads.

Free forever · what wins reviews

  • Feeds, sleep, nappies, burps, tummy time, with timers
  • Timeline, today summary, 24-hour bar
  • Sharing with one partner
  • Milestones, growth charts, KKM vaccination schedule
  • Two tasks with reminders
  • Export to Excel or PDF any time

Belai Premium · RM 69.90/yr · RM 12.90/mo · RM 149 lifetime

  • Predictions: next feed and nap "sweet spot"
  • Growth meaning, weight-for-length, doctor-visit PDF report
  • Unlimited tasks and reminders, unlimited caregivers (grandparents, nanny)
  • Twins and siblings
  • Home-screen widgets and lock-screen timers
  • Card photos, search and full history

Offer a 7-day free trial and show the paywall only after 3 days of use.

Add

  • Real notifications for tasks, medicine and feeds (native apps can do this; the web page cannot)
  • Widgets and lock-screen timers for a running feed or sleep
  • Account sign-in with Apple, Google or email; invite a partner by link
  • Doctor-visit PDF: growth chart, vaccines, 7-day summary
  • Bahasa Melayu, then Chinese and Tamil
  • Medicine, temperature and pumping logs
  • Voice logging ("Hey Siri, log a feed")

Change

  • Onboarding: 3 screens (baby, what to track, invite partner), with sample data you can wipe
  • One "Now" card at the top of Home for whatever is running
  • Cry check becomes "Cry helper": a soothing checklist first, the sound reading as a labelled beta
  • Fold the burp timer into the end of a feed
  • Dim red night theme for 3 am
  • Larger text and screen-reader labels throughout

Remove or hide

  • Manual database setup for sharing: replaced by accounts
  • Excel import: keep export; import only for moving from another app
  • Backup by pasting text: replaced by automatic cloud backup
  • Developer wording ("build", "store", "log id")

Ratings habits

  • Ask for a review at happy moments (a milestone saved, day 7), using the stores' built-in prompt
  • Reply to every review within 2 days
  • Small update every month

Bottom line. RM 10,000 a year is realistic at about 200 paying families, which needs roughly 8,000 downloads a year. The pricing is the easy part; the work is the native app, accounts and steady small releases. Selling one-time only, or launching with the current setup-it-yourself sharing, would make the target much harder.
